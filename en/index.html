Academic profile · Ecuador

Research, data and higher education.

Associate Professor at Universidad Estatal de Milagro (UNEMI), Ecuador. Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Universidad de Sevilla, Spain, awarded Cum Laude with International Distinction. Director of Academic Process Innovation, with over 15 years of experience in university teaching, research, and academic leadership. His work focuses on Business Intelligence, Digital Transformation, Educational Data Mining, and Software Engineering.

Research agenda

Research Lines and Interests

My research focuses on the use of data, digital technologies, and computational methods to improve higher education, support decision-making, and develop software solutions with academic and institutional impact.

Research lines
01

Business Intelligence and Data Analytics

Design of models, indicators, dashboards, and information systems to support academic and organizational decision-making.

02

Educational Data Mining and Learning Analytics

Analysis of student performance, behavior, and trajectories through statistical techniques, data visualization, and machine learning.

03

Programming Education and Computational Thinking

Evaluation of methodologies, tools, and educational modalities to strengthen programming learning and computational competencies.

04

Software Engineering and Recommender Systems

Research on software product lines, feature models, deployment configurations, and intelligent recommender systems.

Research interests
05

Digital Transformation and Innovation in Higher Education

Digitalization, automation, and redesign of academic processes to improve institutional efficiency, traceability, accessibility, and quality.

06

Artificial Intelligence for Education and Decision-Making

Application of machine learning, predictive models, natural language processing, and generative AI in educational and organizational contexts.
